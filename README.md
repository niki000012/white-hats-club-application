### 3. Recent Cybersecurity News / Incident
* **Incident Name:** US Federal Courts & BAE Systems
* **What Happened:** US bankruptcy courts rely on a central system that sends out official notices, such as telling people that a company or person has filed for bankruptcy. That system is run by BAE Systems, a big defense and technology company, not by the courts themselves. BAE was hit by a cyberattack and the system went offline. Notices for every federal bankruptcy court stopped going out. The courts announced this on October 15, and one report says the outage has been going on since October 9. We still don't know what kind of attack it was, whether any data was stolen, or when the system will be back.
* **Source:** https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/cyber-incident-bae-us-bankruptcy/
